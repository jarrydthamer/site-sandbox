SYS_ANOMALY // THE PROBLEM

You pay for an allocation. The unused portion expires.

Most mobile plans include more data than many people use. At the end of a billing cycle, that unused allocation may expire or roll over subject to provider rules. Until it is measured consistently, the gap remains invisible to the customer.

SYS_ENGINE // HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. One verified record.

  1. 1. Keep your plan: Stay with your existing provider. Urbyte does not alter your service, plan or network.
  2. 2. Submit eligible evidence: Provide the bill or recharge evidence needed to compare your included allocation with recorded usage.
  3. 3. Complete the baseline: Complete three eligible billing-cycle conversions across the 84-day baseline. One conversion is processed at a time.
  4. 4. Establish your DBU record: At the fourth eligible conversion, qualifying verified waste becomes an established DBU record. One verified unused GB equals 10 DBUs.
RECORD_SPEC // DBUS

A record of verified waste. Nothing more is implied.

A Data-Backed Unit is an internal, non-transferable Urbyte record derived from qualifying verified allocation waste. DBUs are not cash, cryptocurrency, legal tender, mobile data, stored value or a guaranteed benefit. Supported pathways will be identified only when they are approved and operational.

CORE_POSTURE // WHY URBYTE

SYS_DOCUMENTATION // FAQ

Clear answers.

What does Urbyte verify?
The difference between an eligible plan allocation and the usage recorded for the same billing period.
What is a DBU?
A Data-Backed Unit is an internal, non-transferable platform record. One verified unused GB equals 10 DBUs.
Why is there an 84-day baseline?
Three eligible billing cycles create a more consistent evidence record and reduce the effect of one-off anomalies.
Does Urbyte return mobile data or cash?
No. Urbyte does not control provider networks, return mobile data or guarantee cash or benefits.
Do I need to change providers?
No.
Can prepaid customers participate?
Yes, where eligible recharge evidence shows the included allocation and the unused amount at expiry.
What information is required?
Only the account, allocation, billing-period and usage information needed for verification. Urbyte does not require call, message or content data.
Is Urbyte live?
Consumer sign-up and the current verification pathway are available. Future benefit and Givabit pathways remain subject to approval, funding and operational availability.
