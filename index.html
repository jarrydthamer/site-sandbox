The problem

You pay for an allocation.
The unused portion expires.

Most mobile plans include more data than many people use. At the end of a billing cycle, that unused allocation may expire or roll over subject to provider rules. Until it is measured consistently, the gap remains invisible to the customer.

How it works

Four steps.
One verified record.

01

Keep your plan

Stay with your existing provider. Urbyte does not alter your service, plan or network.

02

Submit eligible evidence

Provide the bill or recharge evidence needed to compare your included allocation with recorded usage.

03

Complete the baseline

Complete three eligible billing-cycle conversions across the 84-day baseline. One conversion is processed at a time.

04

Establish your DBU record

At the fourth eligible conversion, qualifying verified waste becomes an established DBU record. One verified unused GB equals 10 DBUs.

DBUs

A record of verified waste. Nothing more is implied.

A Data-Backed Unit is an internal, non-transferable Urbyte record derived from qualifying verified allocation waste. DBUs are not cash, cryptocurrency, legal tender, mobile data, stored value or a guaranteed benefit. Supported pathways will be identified only when they are approved and operational.
